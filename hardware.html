Hardware Cleaning and Maintenance

  1. Use computer air to clean dust from fans
  2. Wipe the screen gently with a microfiber cloth
  3. Keep liquids away from the keyboard
  4. Ensure proper airflow around the computer
  5. Update system software regularly
